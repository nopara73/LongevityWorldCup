Keep competing

Rejuvenation Olympics is closing.

Its final leaderboard will freeze on 15 April 2027. If you competed there, Longevity World Cup is your chance to keep competing.

Submit your bloodwork, compare your biological age with athletes around the world, and track your progress as you add new results. LWC uses pheno age for Amateur and bortz age for Pro, with Bitcoin prizes for Pro athletes.

LWC is independent, with its own rankings and entry rules. Your RO rank won’t transfer, and DunedinPACE alone won’t qualify. You’ll need a complete clinical blood panel and a fresh application.
