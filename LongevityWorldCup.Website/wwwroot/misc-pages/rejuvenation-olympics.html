Different tests. A fresh entry.

A DunedinPACE result alone does not qualify for LWC. It measures pace of aging using DNA methylation. LWC’s pheno age and bortz age competitions use clinical blood biomarkers to calculate biological age.

Your RO score or rank cannot transfer. Your existing clinical bloodwork might qualify.

Does my bloodwork have what’s needed?

One complete test is enough to apply. Use any laboratory, with every required marker from the same blood draw or report date. Don’t combine results from different dates or guess missing values. You’ll also need your date of birth and the test date.
