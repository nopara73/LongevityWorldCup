Use RDW-CV (%), not RDW-SD (fL). Absolute lymphocyte, neutrophil and monocyte counts need same-test WBC. For CRP below detection, use the lab’s detection limit.

What happens when I apply? +

Calculate with your date of birth, test date and complete panel, then select “Join the leaderboard”. Add your profile, upload lab proof and follow the payment step. We review every application.

Approved profiles and proofs are public. Redact unrelated details before uploading or emailing a report. An email bloodwork check does not submit an application.

Amateur accepts historical tests. Pro accepts tests from January 1–December 31, 2026 and ranks ahead of Amateur in the Ultimate League. Any entry discounts appear during onboarding.

Only have DunedinPACE? You’ll need clinical bloodwork for these tracks. You can join the longevitymaxxing challenge without a blood test.
