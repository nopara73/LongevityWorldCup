# LongevityWorldCup Design Principles

- Preserve the product's playful identity and user-approved copy; follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
- Reuse shared CSS tokens and the established visual system: graphite chrome, neutral surfaces, teal accents, Roboto for functional text, and Orbitron for short competition marks.
- Make meaning clear through layout, grouping, hierarchy, and visual cues before adding labels or explanations.
- Keep layouts readable on desktop and mobile. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, usable touch targets, and states that do not depend on color alone.
- Keep useful content, drafts, and focus stable during loading and background updates. Show the requested view or a quiet loading state; avoid flashes of unrelated defaults.
- Use restrained motion, respect reduced-motion preferences, and keep results and actions immediately available.
