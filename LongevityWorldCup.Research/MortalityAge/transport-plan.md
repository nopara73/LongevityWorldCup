# Mortality age: additional cycle and horizon diagnostics

Specified on 2026-10-09 after the corrected primary models and temporal results were frozen, before calculating these additional endpoint results. The original analysis plan and primary equations remain unchanged. These are diagnostic sensitivities, not a new confirmatory analysis or a basis for retuning or releasing the withheld fitness panel.

The original plan reserves NHANES 2015-2016 for an assay/short-follow-up sensitivity and asks for available longer-time behavior. A completion audit found those quantitative checks missing. Examination-based administrative censoring in the prepared data is at least 2.83 years in 2015-2016 and at least 14.75 years in 2003-2004. Those follow-up ranges, rather than prediction performance, determine the horizons below.

1. Evaluate the frozen common-core, lipid/inflammation and full marginal integration models in 2015-2016 at **two years**. Use the canonical mg/L values from the newer hs-CRP assay. Apply the existing eligibility and complete-observed-panel definitions and appropriate MEC or morning weights. The full integration remains restricted to ages 18-49, with missing inputs marginalized using the existing development distributions. This is neither complete-panel validation nor an isolated assay comparison: population, calendar period, selection and measurement methods also differ.
2. Evaluate every available frozen observed panel and the full marginal integration in 2003-2004 at **ten years**. Enforce the same panel/age eligibility and weights. Record unavailable panels explicitly. This checks sensitivity to longer follow-up using an already inspected temporal cohort and cannot be called independent new validation.
3. For each supported comparison record participants, total deaths, deaths by the horizon, median and minimum administrative follow-up, weighted Kaplan-Meier observed risk, weighted mean expected risk, observed/expected ratio, IPCW Brier error, unweighted Harrell concordance over available follow-up, and horizon-specific Cox recalibration slope when at least 30 horizon deaths occur. Compare age/sex and common-core predictions on the exact same rows and weights. Record sex-specific counts/calibration; zero observed events do not establish calibration.
4. Use 128 PSU-within-stratum bootstrap replicates and seed 91258 for conditional 10th-90th percentile calibration ranges. Keep all outcome models, input distributions, curves, selection and the age reference fixed. These ranges omit model-estimation and transport uncertainty.
5. Verify the diagnostic evaluator against an analytical censoring example and the existing five-year evaluator. Save only derived aggregates with this plan checksum and the model-input checksum. Add the actual results and limitations to the PDF; retain the five-year calculator and its current release status.
