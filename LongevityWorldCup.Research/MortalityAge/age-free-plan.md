# Mortality age: age-free revision, frozen before refitting

Revision 0.2 implements the user's requirement that personal chronological age must not be an input. Version 0.1 and its frozen plans/results remain in Git history. This plan is written before fitting the age-free models. The earlier temporal outcomes have already been inspected; their reuse here is exploratory diagnostic evaluation, not a fresh independent validation.

Remove age, age-by-sex terms and all age-conditioned missing-measurement regressions from every risk model. Their design matrices must be constructible without an age column. Missing biomarkers are conditioned only on sex and the four common-core measurements, retaining the previously declared weighted covariance/dependence assumptions. No estimated age, fixed default age, birth date or age bucket may be substituted into prediction. Preserve sex-dependent biomarker functions. Fit a separate age/sex reference solely to translate predicted five-year mortality into years and as a research benchmark. It never uses the calculator user's age.

Keep the same public data, survey/subsample weighting, preparation, development cycles, respondent hash split, candidate grid, development-only model selection, seeds, support quantiles and sex-specific reference population. Full integration and observed fitness training/evaluation retain the baseline 18-49 cohort; other observed panels retain ages 18-79. Those ranges describe research populations, not required numeric inputs. Removing the field does not establish fitness validity in older adults. State these limits in the calculator/report. Personal measurement date may be retained as draft context; it cannot affect risk or reference inversion.

Recompute all fitted coefficients, missing-input distributions, internal model selection, temporal metrics, survey-bootstrap uncertainty, dependence/integration sensitivities, survivor landmarks and proportional-effects diagnostics. Use the same previously specified 2015 two-year and 2003 ten-year diagnostic endpoints, fixed-model 128-PSU validation intervals, and paired Brier comparisons. No selection or retuning from these reused temporal results. Failed fits/calibration and unfavorable benchmark comparisons must remain reported. Preserve the existing fitness-panel withholding decision and full-panel unvalidated experiment status; no complete jointly observed panel has become available.

Evaluate age-free full and panel predictions against the fixed age/sex reference and age-free common core on exact matching rows/weights. Report sex and age-stratum diagnostics where data permit without adding those strata to prediction. Chronological age is allowed for cohort eligibility, research audit, reference construction and comparisons only. The landmark advances time since examination; it does not insert attained age into the new risk model.

Browser inputs are the nine measurements and recorded sex. Remove birth-date fields, age validation and the above/below chronological-age result. Migrate existing numeric drafts through an explicit whitelist, discarding birth-date values and the legacy draft key. Export schema/version metadata stating that age is not required, exact input names, and research population age ranges separately from the reference range. Regenerate independent Python/browser fixtures and the substantive PDF from saved results. Test predictions with no age column and invariance to arbitrary extra age values, including missing-input quadrature, every observed panel, bootstrap and dependence refits. Test UI migration, alternate units, invalid/unsupported inputs, editing/restoration, desktop/mobile appearance and served artifacts.
